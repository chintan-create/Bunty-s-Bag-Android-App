Privacy Policy — Bunty's Bag

Traditional Handmade Kutchi Crafts Store · Package: com.aistudio.buntysbag.kmxqvp

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Overview

Bunty's Bag ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bunty's Bag Android application collects, how it is used and stored, with whom it is shared, and how you can permanently delete your account and data in compliance with Google Play policies.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Identity Data (Google Sign-In): When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google display name, email address, and unique Firebase User ID to create and authenticate your patron account.
  • Delivery & Contact Information: Recipient name, phone number, street address, city, state, postal code, and optional address label (e.g., Home/Work) that you enter for order fulfillment and shipping.
  • Shopping Activity & Orders: Items in your shopping bag, saved wishlist crafts, gift notes, order totals, order status, and selected payment method.
  • Payment Reference Data: If you choose online payment (Razorpay), checkout is handled by the Razorpay SDK and verified with our payment backend. Bunty's Bag does not collect or store your full card number, CVV, bank credentials, or UPI PIN—only the transaction reference ID (paymentReference) and verification status.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and sync your bag, wishlist, and addresses across sessions.
  • To process, pack, ship, and display the status of your Kutchi craft orders.
  • To verify online payments and prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions.
  • To respond to customer support or account-deletion requests.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and the App contains no ads and no third-party advertising trackers. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure providers required for core app functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Provides Google Sign-In authentication and encrypted cloud database storage isolated to your user account.
  • Razorpay Checkout SDK (when online payment is enabled & selected): Processes UPI, card, netbanking, and wallet transactions securely under Razorpay's privacy and PCI-DSS standards.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All network communication between the App, Firebase, and payment verification endpoints is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Cloud Firestore access is protected by strict authenticated owner-only security rules so only your signed-in account can read or modify your addresses, bag, wishlist, and orders.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and order records only while your account remains active. You may permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

  • In-App Self-Service Deletion: Open Bunty's Bag → Orders tab → Account & Privacy → Delete Account → Delete Permanently. This erases your shopping bag, wishlist, saved addresses, order history, and Firebase Authentication account.
  • Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the App, email cjpania@gmail.com from your registered Google email address to request deletion within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

Bunty's Bag is a retail e-commerce application intended for adults and general shoppers (13+ with guardian supervision where required). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

8. Changes & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
